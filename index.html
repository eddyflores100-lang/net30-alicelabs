Questions, answered

Who can apply?

Any legally registered US business (LLC, corporation, partnership) with at least one supplier reference. New companies without history can start with a prepaid first order and move to Net 30 from the second one.

How much credit can I get?

Starter lines are typically USD 500-2,500 depending on references and registration status. Every cycle paid on time increases your line at review.

What does it cost?

Nothing if you pay within 30 days — no interest, no fees. Invoices unpaid past 30 days accrue a 1.5% monthly service charge (18% APR) or the legal maximum, whichever is lower.

How do I get the invoice?

By email, through Stripe Invoicing, with the due date 30 days after issue and automatic reminders before and after. You pay online by card or ACH.

How long does approval take?

We reply within 1-2 business days of receiving a complete application. If we need a quick call with one of your references, we may ask — it adds a day at most.
